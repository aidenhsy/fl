# Handii Personas

> **Purpose:** Ground product, design, and engineering decisions in a shared understanding of who we're building for. Anchor vertical is **photography**; personas should generalize to adjacent services later but aren't written to do so yet.
>
> **Last updated:** 2026-05-23
> **Owner:** Aiden
> **Status:** v1 — initial draft

---

## Persona Matrix

| | **Client (hires)** | **Seller (provides)** |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | Emma — American tourist | Kenji — full-time pro photographer |
| **Secondary** | Hiroshi — Japanese local | Yuki — side-hustle photographer |

The 2×2 deliberately exercises:
- **Cross-language flows** (Emma ↔ Kenji/Yuki) — validates auto-translation
- **Same-language flows** (Hiroshi ↔ Kenji/Yuki) — validates the JA-first baseline
- **Pro vs. hobbyist seller economics** — different pricing, responsiveness, calendar behavior

## 1. Emma Chen — The American Tourist

![Persona: Client / Tourist]

### Demographics & Language
- **Age:** 32
- **Location:** San Francisco, CA — visiting Tokyo for 6 days
- **Occupation:** Marketing manager at a mid-size SaaS company
- **Languages:** English (native), ~20 words of Japanese ("arigatou," "sumimasen")
- **App language:** EN
- **Device:** iPhone 15, iOS 18, heavy app user

### Goals & Jobs-to-be-Done
- Get **professional vacation photos** in iconic Tokyo locations (Senso-ji, Shibuya crossing, teamLab) without holding up her travel companion or fumbling with a tripod
- Book within **2–3 days of arrival**, ideally before she lands
- Walk away with **edited photos delivered within a week** for Instagram and a printed photobook
- Feel **safe and confident** booking a stranger in a country where she doesn't speak the language

### Pain Points & Frustrations
- Existing options (Airbnb Experiences, Flytographer) feel **overpriced** ($300–500+) and over-curated
- Direct Instagram outreach to photographers is **awkward** — language barrier, no payment protection, no clear pricing
- Worries about **being scammed** or showing up to a no-show
- Doesn't know how to evaluate Japanese photographer portfolios — what's the going rate? Is this person legit?
- Time zone makes back-and-forth messaging slow

### Tech Savviness & Device Habits
- High — uses Uber, Airbnb, DoorDash, Booking.com fluently
- Books almost everything on mobile; rarely opens a laptop while traveling
- Expects **Apple Pay**, push notifications, real-time chat, in-app translation
- Will abandon a flow that requires more than ~3 minutes or asks for unfamiliar info (e.g., a Japanese phone number)

### Trust & Payment Behaviors
- Needs to see **reviews, portfolio samples, and a clear price upfront** before messaging
- Comfortable paying **before the shoot** if the platform holds funds in escrow
- Strong preference for **credit card / Apple Pay**; will not wire money or use unfamiliar local methods
- Trust signals that matter: review count, response rate, identity verification badge, sample photos that match her aesthetic

### Scenario
> *Three days before her flight, Emma is on the train home scrolling Instagram. A friend's Tokyo photos remind her she wanted to book a photographer. She opens Handii, filters by "Tokyo · Photography · English-speaking," sorts by rating, and shortlists three sellers. She messages all three with the same question: "Available March 18, 10am, around Asakusa? What's your rate for 2 hours?" Two reply within an hour — in Japanese, auto-translated to English in her chat. She picks the one with the cleaner portfolio, pays the deposit, and adds the shoot to her calendar.*

### Quote
> "I just want to know: are they real, are they good, and how much? If I can see that in 30 seconds, I'll book."

## 2. Hiroshi Tanaka — The Japanese Local

### Demographics & Language
- **Age:** 38
- **Location:** Setagaya, Tokyo
- **Occupation:** Father of two, works at a trading company
- **Languages:** Japanese (native), conversational English
- **App language:** JA
- **Device:** iPhone 14, uses LINE constantly, Rakuten and Mercari heavily

### Goals & Jobs-to-be-Done
- Book a **family photographer** for his daughter's *shichi-go-san* (七五三) at the local shrine
- Find someone **nearby, reasonably priced** (¥15,000–30,000), available on a specific Saturday morning
- Get **natural, candid family photos** — not stiff studio portraits
- Coordinate logistics in Japanese without ambiguity

### Pain Points & Frustrations
- Studio packages feel **overpriced and formulaic** — same posed shots every family has
- Finding freelance photographers via Instagram or Google requires **vetting effort** he doesn't have time for
- Doesn't want to commit to a stranger without seeing **real reviews from other Japanese families**
- Worried about **last-minute cancellations** on a once-a-year occasion

### Tech Savviness & Device Habits
- Medium-high — comfortable with apps but prefers familiar Japanese services (Rakuten, LINE Pay, Mercari)
- Trusts apps with **clear Japanese UI** and recognizable payment methods
- Uses iPhone exclusively for personal stuff; expects **LINE-quality** chat UX
- Will read **3–5 reviews carefully** before booking; ratings alone aren't enough

### Trust & Payment Behaviors
- Prefers **konbini payment, bank transfer, or credit card** — LINE Pay / PayPay also acceptable
- Comfortable with **deposit + balance on the day** model
- Needs **specific identity signals**: real name (not just handle), business registration if claiming professional status, reviews from other Japanese clients
- Suspicious of profiles that feel "too international" or only have foreign-client reviews for a local service

### Scenario
> *Hiroshi's wife reminds him at dinner that *shichi-go-san* is six weeks away. That night he opens Handii, filters "family photographer · Setagaya · weekend availability," and finds Yuki — 4.9 stars, 23 reviews, three of which mention *shichi-go-san*. He messages in Japanese asking about her availability and rate for a 1-hour shoot at Matsuin Shrine. She confirms within the day, sends a sample gallery from a similar shoot last November, and he books on the spot.*

### Quote
> "近所で、ちゃんとした人で、当日来ない心配がない人。それだけです。"
> *(Someone local, someone legit, someone I don't have to worry won't show up. That's all.)*

## 3. Kenji Watanabe — The Full-Time Pro Photographer

### Demographics & Language
- **Age:** 41
- **Location:** Nakano, Tokyo
- **Occupation:** Self-employed commercial & editorial photographer (8 years freelance, 5 years agency before that)
- **Languages:** Japanese (native), business-level English, basic Mandarin
- **App language:** JA
- **Device:** iPhone 15 Pro + iPad Pro for portfolio review; MacBook for editing

### Goals & Jobs-to-be-Done
- **Fill calendar gaps** between commercial gigs with shorter consumer shoots (couples, tourists, families)
- Build a **direct client pipeline** that doesn't depend on agencies or Instagram's algorithm
- Charge **his actual rate** (¥40,000–80,000 for 2 hours) without being undercut by hobbyists
- Get paid **quickly and reliably** — no chasing invoices

### Pain Points & Frustrations
- Marketplace platforms tend to **race to the bottom** on price; doesn't want to compete with ¥10,000 hobbyists
- Instagram leads are **flaky** — 60%+ don't convert, lots of "how much?" with no follow-through
- Hates being asked to **discount or do test shoots**
- Cross-language clients are great revenue but **drain time** in pre-booking chat

### Tech Savviness & Device Habits
- High — uses Adobe CC, Capture One, Notion, Stripe, Squarespace
- Expects **calendar sync, automated booking confirmations, structured briefs** (location, headcount, deliverable count)
- Will abandon a platform that adds friction to his actual workflow (e.g., forces him to message in-app when he'd rather sync to email)
- **Push notifications matter** — he checks during gigs and needs to know if a high-value lead came in

### Trust & Payment Behaviors
- Wants **escrow or platform-held payment** so he never has to chase
- Comfortable with **platform fees up to ~15%** if it brings qualified leads
- Will list a **clear rate card** in his profile and won't negotiate below it
- Cares about **client verification** — wants to know the booking is real before blocking his calendar

### Scenario
> *Kenji finishes an editorial shoot at 3pm with nothing booked for the rest of the week. He checks Handii — a notification came in 20 minutes ago: a tourist from San Francisco asking about a 2-hour Asakusa shoot in 4 days. He opens the thread, sees her message auto-translated to Japanese, glances at her verified profile, and replies in Japanese with his rate and two time slot options. He gets the booking confirmation that evening and the deposit hits his Stripe Connect account the next morning.*

### Quote
> "値引き交渉が来ない、ちゃんと払ってくれる、ドタキャンしない。この3つが揃ってれば最高のプラットフォーム。"
> *(No haggling, reliable payment, no last-minute cancellations. A platform with those three things is the best you can ask for.)*

## 4. Yuki Sato — The Side-Hustle Photographer

### Demographics & Language
- **Age:** 28
- **Location:** Kichijoji, Tokyo
- **Occupation:** UI designer at a fintech startup (Mon–Fri, 10–7); photography on weekends
- **Languages:** Japanese (native), conversational English
- **App language:** JA
- **Device:** iPhone 14, iPad for light editing on the train

### Goals & Jobs-to-be-Done
- Earn **¥30,000–60,000 extra per month** doing 1–2 weekend shoots
- Build a **public portfolio** to maybe go full-time in 2–3 years
- Shoot the kind of work she *wants* — couples, families, candid lifestyle — not corporate headshots
- Keep it **low-stress** — this is a side thing, not her livelihood

### Pain Points & Frustrations
- Inquiries during weekday work hours are **hard to respond to quickly**, and she loses bookings to faster sellers
- Pricing is **stressful** — she undercharges out of imposter syndrome, then resents the workload
- Doesn't want to deal with **complex contracts, tax paperwork, or chargebacks**
- Worried about a **bad review** from a difficult client tanking her ratings before she has volume

### Tech Savviness & Device Habits
- High — designer by trade, comfortable with most apps, opinionated about UX
- Wants the app to **work well on iPhone in spare moments** (lunch break, commute)
- Notifications matter, but she **mutes between 10pm and 8am** and on weekday mornings
- Will read the FAQ and ToS before signing up

### Trust & Payment Behaviors
- Wants **clear, transparent fees** — no surprise deductions
- Comfortable with monthly payouts; doesn't need instant payment
- Prefers the **platform to handle refund disputes** so she's not negotiating directly with upset clients
- Trust signals she values *as a seller*: a verified client base (no fake accounts), clear cancellation policy, review system that lets her respond

### Scenario
> *Sunday morning at 9am. Yuki is at a café in Kichijoji and sees three Handii notifications from overnight: an inquiry from a tourist (auto-translated), a booking confirmation for next Saturday, and a 5-star review from last weekend's couple shoot. She replies to the tourist with her weekend availability in Japanese, accepts the booking, and screenshots the review for her Instagram story. Total time: 4 minutes.*

### Quote
> "本業じゃないから、サクサク返信できて、ちゃんとお金が入って、面倒な交渉がないのが一番。"
> *(It's not my main job, so what matters most is being able to reply quickly, getting paid properly, and not having to deal with messy negotiations.)*

## Cross-Persona Implications for Handii

### Translation (already shipped)
- **Emma ↔ Kenji/Yuki** is the headline cross-language flow. Pre-translate-on-send means Emma sees Kenji's reply in EN instantly when she taps the notification — no loading state, no awkward "translating…" pause.
- **Hiroshi ↔ Kenji/Yuki** is JA↔JA — translation is a no-op, but the same code path runs. Worth confirming the JA→JA case is short-circuited or cached cheaply.

### FCM Push Notifications (next up)
- All four personas check the app via notifications first. Emma needs lock-screen previews **in English**; Hiroshi needs them **in Japanese**; sellers need them **routed by language preference**, not by the sender's language.
- Yuki's quiet-hours behavior argues for **respecting iOS Focus modes** out of the box rather than building custom DND.
- Kenji's "high-value lead" sensitivity suggests notification copy should include **enough context to triage** (location, date, rough scope) without opening the app.

### Pricing & Trust UX
- Kenji and Yuki sit at opposite ends of the **pricing confidence spectrum** — the seller onboarding flow should help Yuki price herself fairly without forcing Kenji through hand-holding.
- Emma and Hiroshi both rely heavily on **reviews as the primary trust signal**. Review volume matters more than rating for early-stage sellers; consider how to surface "new but verified" sellers without burying them.

### Roadmap Hooks
- **Calendar sync** matters most to Kenji (commercial gigs collide with Handii bookings).
- **Stripe Connect / instant payout** matters most to Kenji; **monthly payout** is fine for Yuki.
- **Identity verification badge** matters to Emma and Hiroshi as buyers; matters to Kenji and Yuki as a way to stand out.
